01 — About

What I care about

My research centers on understanding what ML models actually do rather than taking their outputs at face value — from SHAP and KernelSHAP interpretability work to studying what PCA-based debiasing techniques really remove from word embeddings.

Professionally, I've worked as a data analyst on enterprise data warehousing, ELT pipelines, and Medicare claims data, and I build ML applications end to end — from model training through deployment.
